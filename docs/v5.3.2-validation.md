## Remaining bounds

- Q4 quality is model/workload dependent. v5.3.2 makes it the requested
  capacity-first Auto default but does not label it universally lossless;
  Expert mode retains explicit higher-precision choices.
- A file-backed PLE mapping may acquire more resident pages under a long/random
  workload. The UI shows both the complete mapping and the active-row planning
  budget and warns when cold rows may be reclaimed or paged.
- Quick pass remains provisional evidence. Standard/Custom measurement is still
  required before describing a profile as validated.
