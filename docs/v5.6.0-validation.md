Image+DFlash2, stock HIP/Vulkan and scoped Vulkan, was checked with a red
448×448 image, then blue 672×448 and green 448×672 follow-up requests.
Correct perception and integers 1…40, with **152/168** and **133/133** accepted
drafts. The production command builder was tested again without bypass:
protection remains for **b10896 ≤ build < b11319**. The first locally qualified
build is not a claim about untested intermediate tags or all checkpoints.

Agnes/Prism negative loads confirm stock-runtime blocks. DeepSeek V4.1,
unsafe MTP+ngram-mod, newest ROCmFPX Windows compilation and every possible
checkpoint/context remain unqualified; gates were not removed speculatively.

ready RSS remained 34.6/33.8 GiB Vulkan/HIP, comparable to b11249. Mapped-table
savings are not a total-RAM claim. Synthetic OCR/image tests are not general
accuracy benchmarks. All owned inference processes were closed.

## Stock regression and qualified alternative

Direct R9700 ABBA (two runs/build, three internal repeats) comparing b11249
with stock b11319: dense Qwen3.8 pp/tg and HIP dense/MoE remained stable,
while Vulkan Qwen3.6-35B-A3B IQ3_XXS pp512 fell **3417.68 → 2927.80 tok/s
(−14.3%)**; decode stayed stable. Independent b11302 measurements also found
~32% loss at pp128; increasing microbatch did not restore throughput.

A controlled b11319 source-isolation build reversed **only PR #29182,
`94a0ae3e7`**; GDN and all subsequent changes stayed. Throughput recovered
on R9700 (IQ3_XXS, MXFP4, Q6_K) and RX 9070 XT. This established the cause,
not merely a correlation or an AutoTuner launch-code slowdown.

After explicit approval, the maintained **opt-in** build narrows the dispatch
change to Windows/AMD proprietary driver/PCI 1002:7550 or 7551, pins exact
b11319 source and binds source/binary SHA-256 in a receipt. Stock/user settings
are preserved. [Instructions and limits](rdna4-moe-workaround.md).
